    Browser agents work where your team already works.

    They work the way your staff do: sign in, search, read, type, upload. The difference is that every step is recorded, a second agent checks it against your rules, and nothing is submitted until a person on your team says yes.

    Assist Mode

    The agent works alongside a person, who sees every step and can take over at any point.

    The agent runs routines your team has approved, inside your limits. Anything outside them is held for a person.
